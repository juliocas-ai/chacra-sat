## Satellite data to decide what to plant next

ChacraSat helps farmers on Peru's desert northern coast choose crop rotations that save water, rebuild soil and resist El Niño — using real NASA Earth observations.

### How it works

Three simple steps. ChacraSat suggests — the farmer decides.

1

#### Describe your field

Valley, current crop, soil, salinity, irrigation water and what matters most to you.

2

#### See what NASA sees

Temperature, rain and soil wetness from NASA POWER, turned into monthly crop water demand (FAO-56).

3

#### Compare 3 rotations

The best 18-month plans with five scores — soil, water, salinity, El Niño, income — and plain reasons.

### Who it is for

#### Farmers

Small and medium growers in the Moche, Virú, Chao, Chicama and Jequetepeque valleys.

#### Agronomists

Technicians and local agricultural agencies who advise farmers each season.

#### Irrigation boards

Water user associations planning how to share scarce canal water.
